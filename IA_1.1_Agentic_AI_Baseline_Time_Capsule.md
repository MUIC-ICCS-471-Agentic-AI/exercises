# Assignment 1.1: Agentic AI Baseline — Personal Time Capsule

## Assignment Type

Individual baseline reflection / concept check

## Purpose

This assignment records your **current understanding of Agentic AI before the practical work of this course begins**.

There are no model answers. You are not expected to already understand Agentic AI correctly or completely. The goal is to preserve your genuine starting point so that you can compare it with your thinking near the end of the course.

## Duration

Recommended working time: **35–40 minutes**

Complete this assignment **individually during class**.

## Task

Create your own document, copy the questions below into it, and answer each question directly underneath.

Each answer must contain **50–500 words**.

Clear and honest reasoning matters more than polished writing. It is acceptable to be uncertain. For example:

> “I am not sure, but I currently think…”

Do not search for ideal definitions or try to guess what the instructor wants.

### Question 1 — What is Agentic AI?

In your own words, what do you currently think **Agentic AI** or an **AI coding agent** is?

What makes something an *agent* rather than simply an AI tool that gives you an answer?

### Question 2 — How is it different?

How do you think an AI coding agent differs from:

1. asking a conversational AI such as ChatGPT a programming question, and
2. ordinary code autocomplete or code suggestions?

Explain the difference as you currently understand it.

### Question 3 — What would you delegate?

Identify **at least two software-development tasks** that you would currently be comfortable delegating substantially to an AI coding agent.

Briefly explain why.

### Question 4 — What would you keep under human control?

Identify **at least two software-development tasks or decisions** that you would currently be uncomfortable delegating substantially to an AI coding agent.

Explain why you would want a human to remain closely involved.

### Question 5 — The agent says it is finished

Suppose an AI coding agent tells you:

> “The feature is complete. I implemented the requested behavior and all tests pass.”

What, if anything, would you personally check before accepting the work as complete?

Explain why.

### Question 6 — What would count as evidence?

Give **at least three examples** of evidence or checks that would make you more confident that an AI-generated software change is correct and acceptable.

Then identify one thing that could look convincing but might **not be enough by itself**.

### Question 7 — Benefits and risks

What do you currently think will be:

- the **largest benefit** of Agentic AI for professional software developers, and
- the **largest risk or problem**?

Explain your reasoning.

### Question 8 — Your professional future

Imagine yourself working in software development or another computing profession **3–5 years from now**.

What kinds of work do you expect AI agents to perform for you?

What do you think humans will still need to understand, decide, check, or take responsibility for?

### Question 9 — Your personal starting point

Briefly describe your current experience with AI-assisted programming.

Then answer:

**By the end of ICCS471, what would you like to be able to do confidently with Agentic AI that you cannot confidently do today?**
